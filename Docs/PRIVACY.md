# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-21

This privacy policy applies to the Atlas for Mac website at atlas.atomstorm.ai.

## What we collect

The Atlas for Mac website does not collect personal data. It runs Google Analytics in a privacy-first, cookieless configuration (`client_storage` disabled), which counts aggregate page views and referrers and builds no profile of you. Analytics requests are sent to Google's servers for measurement only. We do not use advertising, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking.

The site sets no cookies. The only thing it remembers is your light or dark theme preference, kept in your browser's local storage on your device only. It never reaches us, and we cannot read it.

## The Atlas app

The Atlas for Mac application itself processes system information locally on your device. Its data practices are described separately in the application's documentation.

## Contact

Questions about privacy can go to support@atomstorm.ai.
